# Teardown 2: outline

## Proposed subject

Teardown 1 read our own pricing page and listed three changes we had not made: a VAT line, a link to the proof page and a price summary at the top. Teardown 2 would be the follow-up on the same page: what we changed, what we measured afterwards, and what did not move.

This subject needs no third party's agreement. If you prefer a different public page, name it and confirm that its owner agrees before anything is published.

## Before it can be written

1. Confirm the VAT position of the plan prices so the line can be added without guessing.
2. Make the three changes on the pricing page and note the date.
3. Run the ten-question citation check on the day of the change and again twice at monthly intervals, using the method in the measurement article. Read the trend only after three runs.

## Structure

1. What changed, with the date of each change.
2. The same counts as teardown 1, taken again: words, position of the first price, structured data types, visible FAQ entries.
3. The citation log for the ten questions, run by run, with named and cited kept separate.
4. What moved, what did not, and what we cannot attribute to the changes.
5. What we change next.

## Rules

- Real screenshots of the live page, with numbered annotations.
- No figure without its source or its date.
- If nothing moved, the teardown says so.
