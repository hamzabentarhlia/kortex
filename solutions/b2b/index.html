The problem

Your best thinking lives in places AI engines cannot quote

Webinars, sales calls, conference talks, a founder's LinkedIn posts. The expertise is there, but it is scattered, unstructured and rarely written down as clear answers. Competitors with tidier content get named instead.

What buyers ask AI before they call you

  • What causes [the problem you solve]?
  • [Your approach] vs [the usual alternative]: which is better?
  • Best providers of [your service] for [their industry]
  • How much does [your service] usually cost?
