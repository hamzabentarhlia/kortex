Questions about pricing

Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees or setup costs?

No. KORTEX pricing is fully transparent. Zero setup fees, zero onboarding costs, zero hidden charges. Your invoice equals your plan cost, period. If you upgrade mid-month, we prorate fairly.

Can I change plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade to a higher tier immediately, downgrade at the start of the next billing cycle, or cancel with 30 days notice. No penalties, no fine print.

Who owns the content that KORTEX produces?

You do. 100%. Every article, FAQ block, LinkedIn post and strategic document belongs entirely to your brand from day one. KORTEX has no ownership stake in the content we produce. You can republish, repurpose or resell it without permission.

What if I need more than one source cycle per month?

Upgrade to Swarm (1 cycle included) or Titan (2+ cycles). Or contact our team about custom volume pricing. We can scale with you as your needs grow.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No minimum commitment. You can start with Ghost (1 month), see results, and upgrade to Swarm the next month. Or try Swarm and cancel if it's not right for you. We're confident in the system - we don't need contracts to prove it.

How long is the turnaround on each source cycle?

Ghost and Swarm: 5 business days from submission to delivery. Titan: 3-4 business days with priority processing. Once submitted, you get weekly progress updates and a final review before anything goes live.

Do I get support if something's wrong?

Ghost and Swarm get email support (48-hour response). Titan gets dedicated Slack support with priority response. If an asset needs revision, we refactor it at no additional cost. Our goal is your satisfaction with the system.

Can I request custom pricing or a trial period?

Yes. Reach out to us directly at contact@kortexlab.me and we'll discuss your specific situation. We offer free gap analysis for all new inquiries - no commitment, no pitch call required.
