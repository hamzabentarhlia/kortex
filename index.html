What is Generative Engine Optimization?

Search is changing. Buyers now ask Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and their peers to explain a category before they ever visit a vendor site. KORTEX helps B2B SaaS teams turn their strongest existing thinking into content that is easier for people and retrieval systems to understand.

That does not mean publishing more generic blog posts. It means making your category, point of view, products, and proof clear across a connected set of useful pages and distribution assets.

We call this Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): a content architecture that improves your chances of being understood, retrieved, and attributed when someone asks a question in your category.

Common questions

What B2B founders ask before they start.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes content to rank high in search results by targeting keywords and building backlinks. Your goal is page one of Google. GEO optimizes content to be cited by AI answer engines. Your goal is being the source ChatGPT or Perplexity names when someone asks a question in your category. SEO brings clicks. GEO brings authority and qualified referral traffic from inside AI-generated answers.

How is KORTEX different from a traditional content agency?

Traditional agencies produce outputs - blog posts, social content, newsletters. They measure success by volume. KORTEX builds infrastructure. Every asset is architecturally connected, entity-optimized and designed to compound over time. We measure success by citation authority and qualified lead velocity. The goal is not content volume. It is being the brand AI systems retrieve.

What do you need from us to get started?

One strong source: a founder video, podcast, webinar or demo recording. Minimum 20-30 minutes of real thinking on a topic you own. Not a polished marketing video - your raw intellectual material. We extract the commercial logic, structure the entity graph and produce the full asset stack from there. The better the source, the stronger the system.

How long does it take to see citation results?

The structural foundation - entity clarity, pillar articles, FAQ clusters and internal linking - is in place within the first source cycle, typically 4-6 weeks. Citation visibility inside AI engines typically begins to move within 6-8 weeks because the architecture targets retrieval logic rather than slow-building backlink graphs. Authority compounds with each additional source cycle.

Who is KORTEX built for?

B2B SaaS founders, expert consultants and operator-led teams that already produce high-value long-form content - webinars, demos, podcasts, videos - but whose thinking disappears before it compounds into authority. We serve companies that want to be cited as sources, not just get traffic. If your insight is strong but your infrastructure is weak, KORTEX fixes that.

What about my current blog posts and content?

We can audit your existing content and identify where it lacks entity clarity, answer-first formatting or internal link authority. Some pieces can be retrofitted into the GEO system. Others should be retired because they don't reinforce your category thesis. The goal is a coherent authority graph, not maximizing total page count.
