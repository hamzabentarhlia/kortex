Choosing your source

What makes a strong video to start from

Talking-head content, talks and tutorials work best. Pure entertainment or vlogs carry less reusable expertise.

  • {{icon:check}}You explain something in depth
  • {{icon:check}}It runs 15 minutes or more
  • {{icon:check}}It contains opinions, not only facts
  • {{icon:check}}You can share the link or the file
Questions

Questions about videos

Do you need the original file?

A public or unlisted link is enough. If the video is private, you can share the file instead.

Can you use an interview where I am the guest?

Yes, as long as you are comfortable building content from what you said in it.
