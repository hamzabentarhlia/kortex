Methodology

The 40-question test

The same test we run in the AI Citation Audit, run on ourselves.

1

Forty buyer questions

Questions a real buyer would type, in full sentences, grouped by buying stage. We freeze the wording so a change between runs means something.

2

Four engines

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. For each answer we log the date, the engine, the surface we used, and whether an AI answer was shown at all.

3

Named and cited, separately

An answer can mention us without linking us, or link us without naming us. We log both, and the other sources cited, because that column shows who owns the answer.

How we read it

Answers vary by engine and by day, and Google says AI Overviews often do not appear at all. So we never judge one run. We read trends over at least three runs, and we note what we changed on the site between them. We do not publish a single blended score.
