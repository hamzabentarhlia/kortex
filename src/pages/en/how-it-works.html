Step by step

What happens at each stage

Each stage has one job. Here is what it produces and what we need from you.

  1. 01 Source

    You send one link

    A blog post, YouTube video, podcast episode or webinar recording. The richer it is in opinions, examples and methods, the better. A short intake form tells us your offer, your buyers, your competitors and the words you never want to use.

  2. 02 Extract

    We pull the expertise out

    The source is transcribed in full. Agents list every claim, example, number, objection and question you answer, with a pointer back to where you said it. This list is the raw material for everything that follows, so nothing gets invented later.

  3. 03 Structure

    We map it to real questions

    We match your ideas to the questions buyers ask AI engines in your field, and decide which page answers which question. We also fix the vocabulary: your company, your method and your category get one name each, used everywhere.

  4. 04 Produce

    Agents draft each asset

    Articles, FAQ pages, comparison pages, glossary entries, LinkedIn and social posts, newsletters and short video scripts. Each agent works on one format and follows the same plan, so the pieces link together and say the same thing.

  5. 05 Review

    An editor checks, you approve

    A person reads every asset against the extraction list. Any claim we cannot trace to your source is flagged for you, not published. You get everything in a shared document and approve or comment.

  6. 06 Publish

    Live with the right structure

    Pages ship answer-first, with headings, tables and schema markup that machines can parse, and with internal links between them. You publish yourself, or we connect to your CMS on TITAN.

  7. 07 Measure

    We check whether it works

    We ask a fixed set of buyer questions to the main AI engines and record who gets cited. The findings decide what the next month of content covers.

Beyond your website

AI engines also listen to what others say about you

When an engine decides whom to cite, it checks whether other trusted sites agree. So part of the work happens off your site: reviews, mentions, directory listings and a consistent profile on the third-party platforms these engines read.

{{icon:plus}}Go deeper: what we mean by off-site authority

An entity is a thing a machine can identify without doubt: your company, your founder, your product. Engines build their picture of an entity from many sources. If your website, your LinkedIn page, your directory listings and the articles that mention you all describe you the same way, the picture is clear and you are easier to recommend.

We audit those sources, fix inconsistent descriptions, and help you earn mentions on platforms that engines already trust in your field.

Questions

Questions about the method

How much of my time does this take?

The intake form at the start, then reading and approving drafts during the month. We do the rest.

Which AI tools does the pipeline use?

Several language models and automation tools, each picked for a specific job such as transcription, extraction or drafting. The tools change as better ones appear. The method and the human review stay the same.

Will the content sound like me?

That is the point of starting from your own material. We keep your vocabulary, your examples and your opinions, and we build a voice guide from your source so every asset uses it.

Do you publish the content for me?

We deliver assets ready to publish. On TITAN we can connect to your CMS and publish for you. Posting on your social accounts stays with your team.
