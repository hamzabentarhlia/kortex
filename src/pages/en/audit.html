The problem

Your buyers ask AI before they ask you

A buyer with a long question rarely starts on your site. They ask an assistant, read the answer, and arrive with a shortlist. You cannot see that conversation, and the answer changes by engine and by day. The only way to know what AI says about your field is to ask it, in the buyer's words, and write down what comes back.

{{icon:eye}}

You cannot see it from analytics

Search Console reports Google's AI features. It says nothing about ChatGPT, Perplexity or what a buyer was told before they clicked.

{{icon:target}}

The answer names someone

If an answer names a competitor and not you, that is a fact you can act on, and one most teams have never looked at.

{{icon:layers}}

The cause is usually fixable

Pages that do not answer the question, a blocked crawler, a confused entity. A good audit names the cause, not just the symptom.

Questions about the audit

Do I have to buy a plan afterwards?

No. The audit stands alone and you keep the report. If you do start a plan, the €490 is credited in full against your first month.

Which engines do you test?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. For each answer we log the engine, the surface we used, whether you were named and cited, and which sources were cited instead.

Does the audit guarantee I will be cited?

No. AI engines are third-party systems and none publishes a full recipe for being cited. The audit shows where you stand today and what to change first.

What do I need to send?

Your name, company and website. If you know your main competitors or have a list of questions your buyers ask, send them, but you do not need to.

What if the engines already cite me?

Then the report says so, shows which pages are working and why, and the plan focuses on the questions where you are still absent.

Why a fit check before payment?

The audit works best for companies whose buyers research a purchase before they talk to sales. A short check tells us, and you, whether it is worth doing.
