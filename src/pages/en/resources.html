How we write

How we source and check what we publish

Primary sources only

Statements about how an engine works come from that engine's own documentation. If we could not open the page, we do not cite it, and where an engine publishes nothing we say so.

Labelled practice

What we do ourselves is labelled as our practice. We separate it from what an engine documents, because a habit that worked for a client is not a rule.

A person checks every claim

AI helps with drafts and structure. A person checks each figure, name and statement before anything goes live.

Dated and updated

Each article shows when it was published and last updated. Guidance in this field changes, and a page that has not been reviewed should say so.
