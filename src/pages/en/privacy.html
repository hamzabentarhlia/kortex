Who we are

KORTEX is a content and GEO studio based in Fès, Morocco. For any privacy question, write to @@email.

What we collect

  • Form data. When you book a call or write to us, we receive your name, email address, the link you share and any message you write.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep the conversation.
  • Usage statistics. We measure visits and clicks on buttons to understand which pages help people. See the cookie page for details.

Why we use it

To answer your request, prepare your call, and follow up about it. Statistics help us improve the site. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it for us

  • Web3Forms, which delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Analytics, which measures site usage.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request and any work that follows. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email @@email and we will reply.
