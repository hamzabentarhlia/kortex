Your best article is a cluster waiting to happen

One strong blog post usually answers five to ten questions at once, which makes it hard for an AI engine to quote any of them. We split it into focused pages, each answering one question clearly, and link them together.

Choosing your source

What makes a strong blog post to start from

The best starting point is not your most popular post but your most opinionated one.

  • {{icon:check}}It takes a clear position
  • {{icon:check}}It explains how you do something, not only what
  • {{icon:check}}It includes real examples or numbers
  • {{icon:check}}It is at least 1,200 words long
Questions

Questions about blog posts

Will the original article change?

We suggest a restructured version that answers the main question first. You decide whether to replace the original or keep both.

What if my article is short?

Short articles work if they are dense. Otherwise we may combine two related posts into one source.
