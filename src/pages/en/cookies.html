Before you choose

The site loads no analytics and sets no cookies. A small banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Accept and Decline do the same amount of work for you: the site works the same either way.

Google Analytics and Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Every page starts with analytics and advertising storage set to denied. The Google Analytics script (gtag.js from googletagmanager.com) is only downloaded after you click Accept. Advertising storage stays denied in every case: we do not use Google Analytics for advertising.

If you decline, the script is never downloaded. If you accepted earlier and now decline, we stop analytics and remove the _ga cookies that this site can reach.

The booking calendar

The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com. It only loads when you open it from a booking button or scroll to it on the contact page. Cal.com may store its own cookies or local data under its privacy policy.

Change your choice

Use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page. You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser at any time.
