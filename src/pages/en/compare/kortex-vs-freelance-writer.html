What a good freelancer brings

Craft, a human voice, and often deep knowledge of a niche. A great writer who knows your field is hard to beat for flagship pieces.

When a freelancer is the better choice

When you need a handful of deeply researched flagship articles, or a very particular literary voice, and you have time to brief and review each piece.

When not to choose KORTEX

  • You only need two or three articles a month.
  • You want a writer to interview you weekly and craft long narrative pieces.
  • You already have a great writer and a good repurposing process.

Can they work together?

Yes. Some clients keep their writer for flagship pieces and use KORTEX to turn each one into everything else.
