In short

An SEO agency mainly improves how your existing pages rank in search results. KORTEX produces new content from your own expertise and shapes it to be quoted inside AI answers, with technical and on-page SEO included. If your content is already strong and only needs ranking work, an SEO agency may be enough.

What does each one actually do?

An SEO agency audits your site, fixes technical issues, researches keywords, builds links and often writes or briefs content around search volume. Its main scoreboard is rankings and organic traffic.

KORTEX starts from a piece of your own content, extracts the expertise in it, and produces a month of assets across formats: articles, FAQ pages, comparisons, glossary entries, social posts. Each one is structured so AI answer engines can quote it, and each page ships with the technical SEO basics done.

When is an SEO agency the better choice?

If you already publish strong content regularly and your problem is technical (slow site, indexing errors, a migration), a specialised SEO agency is the right call. The same applies if you need large-scale link building in a very competitive market.

When is KORTEX the better choice?

If your expertise exists but sits in videos, talks or a few long posts, and buyers in your field already ask AI engines for advice, the bottleneck is content, not rankings. That is the problem KORTEX solves.

When not to choose KORTEX

  • You have no source material and no expert willing to provide one.
  • You want hundreds of keyword pages at the lowest cost per word.
  • Your main issue is a broken website that needs engineering work first.

Can you use both?

Yes, and many teams should. An SEO agency keeps the site healthy. KORTEX fills it with content worth citing. We are happy to work alongside your agency.
