Before you decide

Pricing questions

Why is this more than a freelance writer?

A writer delivers articles. A KORTEX month delivers a connected set of assets across formats, the structure and markup that make them quotable, an editor who checks every claim, and on TITAN the tracking that shows whether AI engines cite you. Compare it with the combined cost of a writer, an SEO specialist and someone to repurpose content for social.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. There is no commitment and no setup fee. Plans run month to month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Many teams start with GHOST or SWARM and move up once they see which topics work. Tell us before the next month starts.

Which plan should I start with?

One source a month in a quiet niche: GHOST. One source a month and competitors already showing up in AI answers: SWARM. Several sources a month, several brands, or a category to own: TITAN. Not sure? The free audit ends with a recommendation.

What is not included?

Recording your source, running your social accounts, and paid advertising. We also do not guarantee that a specific AI engine will cite you, because no one controls those systems.

Can you guarantee results?

No honest provider can guarantee citations. We guarantee the work: a month of reviewed assets built to be cited, and on TITAN a clear record of where you are and are not cited.
