Before you decide

Pricing questions

Why is this more than a freelance writer?

A writer delivers articles. A KORTEX month delivers a connected set of assets across formats, the structure and markup that make them quotable, an editor who checks every claim, and a monthly check of whether AI engines cite you. Compare it with the combined cost of a writer, an SEO specialist and someone to repurpose content for social.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything we produce belongs 100% to you from the day it is delivered.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Plans run month to month with 30 days' notice and no penalty. There is no setup fee.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated for the current month. Downgrades apply from the next billing cycle.

Which plan should I start with?

One source a month in a quiet niche: GHOST. One source a month with competitors already in AI answers: SWARM. Several sources, several brands or a category to own: TITAN. The free audit ends with a recommendation.

Can you guarantee results?

No honest provider can guarantee citations. We guarantee the work: reviewed assets built to be cited, and a monthly record of where you are cited and where you are not.

Assumptions behind the default numbers

  • The defaults are KORTEX working assumptions for the full monthly employer cost of experienced full-time profiles. They do not come from a salary survey and vary a lot by country, city and seniority.
  • Hiring, software, training and management time are left out, so the real in-house cost is probably higher.
  • The scope differs: an in-house team can handle more than one source a month and run your community, which KORTEX plans do not include. This compares budgets, not identical output.
  • No commercial result or citation is assumed. This is a cost comparison only.
