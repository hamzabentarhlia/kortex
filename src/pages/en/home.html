Why this matters now

Buyers ask AI first. AI quotes a handful of sources.

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview about your field, they get one written answer that names only a few sources. GEO, short for Generative Engine Optimization, is the work of making your content one of those sources.

{{v:home-collapse}}

Search shows ten links and lets the buyer choose. An AI answer makes the choice for them and names a few sources. If you are not one of them, you are not in the conversation.

{{icon:search}}

SEO gets you found

Search engine optimization makes your pages fast, indexable and relevant, so they rank in the list of results. It still matters: AI engines read the same web.

{{icon:quote}}

GEO gets you quoted

GEO shapes what you publish so an AI engine can lift a clear answer from it and credit you. Direct answers, consistent names, comparisons, facts that other sites confirm.

{{icon:plus}}Go deeper: how an AI engine picks its sources

Most AI answer engines run a search in the background, read the top pages, and write a summary. Pages that answer the question in the first lines, define their terms, and say the same thing as other trusted sites are easier to quote. Pages that bury the answer under an introduction usually get skipped.

So GEO is mostly editorial discipline: answer first, name things the same way everywhere, back claims with specifics, and be present on the third-party sites these engines trust, such as review platforms, directories and industry publications.

Questions

What people ask before booking a call

What is GEO, in one sentence?

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring your content so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand it, quote it and name you as the source.

Do I need to record videos?

No. A strong blog post, a podcast episode or a recorded webinar works just as well. What matters is that the source contains real expertise: opinions, examples, methods, numbers.

Is this AI-generated content?

AI agents do the drafting. The ideas come from your source, an editor checks every claim against it, and nothing is published until you approve it. Anything we cannot trace back to your material is flagged for you instead of being made up.

Can you guarantee that AI engines will cite me?

No one can honestly guarantee that. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google decide what they cite. We control how clear, consistent and quotable your content is, and we measure what actually happens so you can judge the results yourself.

How is this different from an SEO agency?

An SEO agency mostly works on rankings in the list of results. We cover the technical and on-page SEO too, but we also build the content itself from your expertise and shape it to be quoted inside AI answers.

What if I want to stop?

Every plan is month to month with no setup fees. You stop with 30 days' notice and no penalty.
