How they affect visibility. h1: Google AI Overviews summary: Google's AI summary above the search results. kind: term kicker: Glossary in_short: Google AI Overviews are AI-written summaries that appear above the regular results for some Google searches. They answer the question directly and show links to the pages they drew from. Being one of those linked sources gives visibility even when fewer people scroll down to the classic list of results. ---

How to appear in them

The same principles as GEO apply: rank reasonably well for the query, answer the question clearly near the top of the page, structure the content, and keep your facts consistent with other trusted sources.
