AI answers make it more common for questions that used to send a visit. ---

In more detail

Direct answers, featured snippets and AI-generated summaries can settle a question on the page where it was asked. The searcher leaves satisfied and the site that supplied the knowledge sees nothing in its traffic. For a publisher this changes what to measure: a page can shape an answer without producing a visit.

Search Console reports impressions and clicks for Google Search, so a growing gap between the two on a page is one place to see it. It does not show what an answer said, which is why a fixed list of questions logged by hand remains the way to see whether your page is the source.

An example

A buyer asks how often suppliers should be reviewed, reads the answer, and moves on to a shortlist without opening a page. If your page is one of the cited sources, it informed the decision. If it is not, a competitor's did.
