Machines build knowledge around entities rather than keywords. When your company, founder and method are described the same way everywhere, engines understand who you are and can recommend you with confidence. ---

Why entities matter for being cited

An AI engine hesitates to cite a source it cannot identify clearly. If your website calls your method one thing, your LinkedIn another, and a directory a third, the picture is blurred. Consistent naming across your site and third-party profiles removes that doubt.

Simple ways to make your entity clearer

  • Use one exact name for your company, product and method
  • Describe what you do in the same sentence across profiles
  • Add Organization and Person schema markup to your site
  • Link your official profiles to each other
