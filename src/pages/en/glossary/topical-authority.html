It grows when a site covers that subject in depth, from many angles, with pages that link to each other and say consistent things. It is built by focus, not by volume. --- {{v:glossary-topical-authority}}

How to build it

Choose a narrow subject you truly know. Cover the core questions, the comparisons, the definitions and the objections, each on its own page, and link them together. A small site with complete coverage of one topic often beats a large site with thin coverage of many.
