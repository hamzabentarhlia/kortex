In more detail

The phrase borrows from share of voice. Two teams using it can mean different things: mentions in text, links to a site, or a weighted score. We use a plain version. Fix a list of buyer questions, run it in each engine, and count the questions where each brand is cited, then divide by the number of questions. Keep it per engine and per month, and keep "named" and "cited" as separate counts.

Answers vary from run to run and across product surfaces, so a score with decimals built on ten questions is false precision. Read the trend over at least three runs.

An example

On a list of ten questions, your site is cited for three in one engine and a competitor's for five. Your count is three out of ten and theirs five out of ten. The competitor column in the log shows which of their pages do the work.
