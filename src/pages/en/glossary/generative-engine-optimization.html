Definition and examples. h1: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) summary: Making your content the source AI answers quote. kind: term kicker: Glossary in_short: Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, is the practice of structuring content so that AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand it, quote it and name you as the source. It focuses on clear answers, consistent naming and facts other sites confirm, rather than on ranking a page in a list. ---

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank a page in the list of search results. GEO aims to be selected and cited inside the single answer an AI engine writes. GEO builds on good SEO, because most engines search the web before answering, but it changes how you write and how you measure success.

What does GEO work look like in practice?

  • Opening each page with a direct answer to one question
  • Naming your company, product and method the same way everywhere
  • Adding comparisons, definitions and FAQs that are easy to quote
  • Being described consistently on third-party sites engines trust
  • Tracking whether engines actually cite you
