title: What is llms.txt? | KORTEX glossary description: llms.txt is a proposed plain-text file at the root of a website that gives language models a short description of the site and links to its most useful pages. h1: llms.txt summary: A simple file that introduces your site to AI models. kind: term kicker: Glossary in_short: llms.txt is a proposed plain-text file placed at the root of a website, for example example.com/llms.txt. It gives language models a short description of the site and a list of its most useful pages with one-line summaries. It is a voluntary convention, so treat it as a helpful extra, not a guarantee. ---

Should you add one?

It takes minutes and does no harm. Keep it short, factual and up to date, and list the pages you would want an AI to read first. This site has one.
