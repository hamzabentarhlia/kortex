Why it helps AI and search. h1: Schema markup summary: Labels in your code that tell machines what a page contains. kind: term kicker: Glossary in_short: Schema markup is structured data, usually written in JSON-LD, that you add to a web page to tell machines exactly what it contains: an article and its author, a product and its price, an FAQ and its answers, an organization and its profiles. It helps search engines and AI systems read your content without guessing. --- {{v:glossary-schema-markup}}

Which types matter most

  • Organization and Person, to define who you are
  • Article or BlogPosting, with author and dates
  • FAQPage, for question and answer sections
  • Product and Offer, for e-commerce
  • BreadcrumbList, for site structure
