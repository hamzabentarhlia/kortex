It is an SEO community term, close to what Google's guidance calls originality and added value. ---

In more detail

Google's description of main content quality lists originality, the extent to which content offers unique, original information or perspectives that are not already available on other websites. It also says that attribution to other sources does not replace the need for original effort. A page that only restates what is online gives an engine no reason to pick it over the others, and a page with its own data or case does.

An example

Ten pages explain what a supplier risk score is. One of them includes the way a real company's team reads a score, a figure from its own review and the objection its customers raise most. That page has information gain. The other nine have a definition.

Sources of gain you already hold: your own measurements, a method you actually use, the questions your sales team hears, and examples from your work.
