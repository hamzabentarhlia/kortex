How it is measured and why it varies. h1: Citation rate summary: How often AI answers name you for the questions that matter. kind: term kicker: Glossary in_short: Citation rate is the share of a fixed set of questions for which an AI answer engine names or links your brand. For example, if you track 20 buyer questions and ChatGPT cites you in 5 answers, your citation rate on ChatGPT is 25 percent for that set. It is the main way to measure GEO progress. ---

How to measure it reliably

Pick 10 to 30 questions your buyers really ask. Run them in each engine on a regular schedule. Record whether you are named, linked, or absent, and who is cited instead. Answers vary between runs, so look at the trend over several months, not a single result.
