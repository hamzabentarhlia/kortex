In more detail

AI answer engines and Google's AI features surface links next to or inside their answers. Those links are the citations. A buyer can click one to verify a claim or read more, which makes a citation the closest thing an AI answer has to a visit. Each engine shows citations its own way, and the same question can show different sources on different days.

An example

A buyer asks which approach to supplier risk suits a mid-size manufacturer. The answer names three tools in its text and links four pages. The four linked pages are cited. A company named in the text without a link is named, and a company that is both named and linked is named and cited. The two outcomes are logged in separate columns.

Google states that its AI features surface relevant links to help people find information and explore content, and that a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet to be a supporting link.
