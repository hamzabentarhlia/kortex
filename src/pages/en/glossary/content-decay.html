In more detail

A page can decay in three ways: its facts go out of date, a better page appears for the same question, or the question itself changes. For citations the first matters most. A figure from two years ago that sits on five pages makes each of them a less safe source to quote. A review date on every asset, and a reason to revisit it, is the simple defence.

When you update a page, change the content and the visible date in the same edit. A date that moves without any change behind it misleads readers and weakens the signal.

An example

A comparison page states a price that was correct in 2024. Nobody revisits it. By 2026 the figure is wrong and a competitor's page has the current one. A quarterly review would have caught it, and the correction would have shown as a dated update.
