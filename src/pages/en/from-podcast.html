What makes a strong episode to start from

Solo episodes and expert interviews work best. Casual chat episodes work if they contain clear advice.

  • {{icon:check}}One main topic, explored in depth
  • {{icon:check}}Practical advice, not only stories
  • {{icon:check}}30 minutes or more
