Why I started KORTEX

My background is in customer retention, business intelligence and automation. In retention you learn quickly that people stay with the company that answers their question at the right moment. In business intelligence you learn that the answer has to be clear enough to act on. In automation you learn that a good process can do in hours what used to take weeks.

Then I watched how people started looking for answers. They stopped scrolling through ten links. They asked ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overview, and they trusted the few sources named in the reply.

Meanwhile the experts I knew were sitting on hours of valuable material: talks, podcasts, webinars, long articles. Almost none of it was in a shape these engines could quote. So their competitors, often with less to say, were the ones getting named.

KORTEX is the system I built to fix that. It takes what an expert already said, pulls out the substance, and rebuilds it in every format that buyers and AI engines read. Agents do the volume. People check the facts. The expert stays the author.

What I believe

  • {{v:about-b1}}Expertise cannot be generated.

    AI is good at reshaping knowledge and bad at inventing it. So we start from yours.

  • {{v:about-b2}}Clarity beats volume.

    Ten pages that answer real questions do more than a hundred that circle around them.

  • {{v:about-b3}}A person signs off.

    Anything published in your name should be read by someone who cares about getting it right.

  • {{v:about-b4}}Measure what is real.

    If we say content is built to be cited, we should check whether it is.

  • {{v:about-b5}}Say when we are not the right fit.

    It costs us a deal now and saves everyone time.

Our mission

Make sure that when someone asks an AI engine about a field, the people who actually know that field are part of the answer.
