How to use it

Score yourself in an afternoon

Give each check a pass, a partial or a fail. Fix the failures in the Access group first, then Pages. A pass on every check does not guarantee you will be cited, because no engine publishes a full recipe, but a fail on the first group means the rest cannot work.
