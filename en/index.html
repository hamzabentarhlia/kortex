What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content so that answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand it, retrieve it and attribute it to you when they generate an answer.

Search is changing. B2B buyers now ask Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and their peers to explain a category, compare vendors and shortlist options before they ever visit a vendor site. Often they never see ten blue links: they read one synthesized answer that names a few sources. If your company is not one of those sources, you are not part of the conversation.

GEO does not mean publishing more generic blog posts. It means making your category, point of view, product and proof unambiguous across a connected set of useful pages and distribution assets: clear definitions, answer-first paragraphs, consistent entity names, comparison tables, FAQs and internal links that tell a machine how your ideas relate.

KORTEX applies GEO to the raw material B2B founders already have: their own long-form thinking. That is why we start from a founder video rather than a keyword list. Read the full argument in why GEO replaces ranking as the goal of B2B content.

GEO vs SEO

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO optimizes pages to rank in a list of search results; GEO optimizes content to be selected, summarized and cited inside an AI-generated answer. They are complementary: GEO builds on technically sound, indexable pages, but it changes what you write, how you structure it and how you measure success.

The problem with B2B content in 2026

Why does your content rank while
competitors get cited?

Because most B2B content is built to be found, not to be quoted. When a buyer asks ChatGPT for "the best AI content agency for B2B SaaS", they get a short answer that names a handful of sources. Those sources are rarely the companies with the most blog posts; they are the companies whose content is clear, consistent and easy for a model to extract.

Content that expires in 48 hours

Teams spend days producing one post, then watch the feed move on. There is no retrieval layer, no compounding memory and no connection to a commercial thesis. LinkedIn and search see the piece once, then forget it. Read why B2B LinkedIn content dies so fast.

Invisible to AI answer engines

Answer engines favour content they can understand and extract: precise entities, direct answers, structured comparisons. Generic content gets synthesized around rather than cited. Your insight ends up blended into the model's output with no attribution to you.

Insight trapped in the wrong format

Founders produce high-value thinking every week in webinars, demos, sales calls and podcasts. Most of it is never written down, structured or published. The best raw material already exists; it just needs infrastructure to become authority instead of noise.

Fragmented message, no authority graph

Publishing across LinkedIn, email and the blog without a shared entity structure creates activity, not authority. The market sees motion; models see inconsistent signals. There is no coherent picture of who you are and what you are the answer to.

This scenario shows the mechanism, not a promise of citations. No agency can guarantee that an answer engine will cite a specific page; what GEO controls is how clear, consistent and extractable your expertise is.

What do B2B founders ask before they start?

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content so AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand, retrieve and attribute it. Unlike traditional SEO, GEO focuses on how content is summarized and referenced inside AI-generated answers: clear definitions, consistent entities, answer-first formatting and evidence.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes content to rank high in search results through keywords, backlinks and technical health; the goal is page one of Google. GEO optimizes content to be cited by AI answer engines; the goal is being a source ChatGPT or Perplexity names when someone asks a question in your category. SEO brings clicks; GEO builds authority inside the answer itself. The two work best together.

What does KORTEX do?

KORTEX is a GEO and AI content automation agency that converts one founder video or long-form source into a 30-day authority system: pillar articles, FAQ clusters, comparison pages, LinkedIn distribution assets and an internal linking architecture, designed to make a B2B company's expertise easier for buyers and answer engines to understand.

How is KORTEX different from a traditional content agency?

Traditional agencies produce outputs (blog posts, social content, newsletters) and often measure success by volume. KORTEX builds a system: every asset is connected, entity-consistent and designed to compound over time.
