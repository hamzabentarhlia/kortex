The process

What happens after you submit?

Three steps, all async: we review your source within 48 hours, run an audit, then send a written proposal. No sales call is required — you can ask for one if you prefer.

  1. 48-hour review

    We watch or listen to your source and check that it holds enough signal — a clear point of view, a mechanism, real examples — to build a full authority cycle. If it doesn't, we tell you what to record instead.

  2. The audit

    We map your core message, the category you can own and the questions buyers ask answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. We look at how your current site would be read by those engines and outline the first authority cluster to build.

  3. The proposal

    You receive a written 30-day plan — pillar, contrarian article, comparison page, FAQ cluster, distribution — and the plan we recommend (GHOST, SWARM or TITAN). If it fits, we start the first source cycle; if not, you keep the audit.

Contact FAQ

What should I send?

One link to the recording that best captures how you think: a webinar, podcast episode, conference talk, sales demo or a Loom where you explain your point of view. 45–60 minutes is ideal, but a shorter recording with strong opinions works too.

Does the video need to be professionally produced?

No. The recording is a source document, not something we publish as is. Raw, unedited footage is fine — what matters is the substance: your position, your mechanism and your examples.

What if I don't have a recording yet?

Say so in the message field and add any link to your site or LinkedIn profile instead. We'll send you a short list of questions to answer in a 45–60 minute Loom or video call, which becomes your first source.

How quickly will you reply?

Within 48 hours. Every submission is reviewed and answered from contact@kortexlab.me, including when the source isn't a fit.

Do I have to get on a sales call?

No. KORTEX works asynchronously: the review, the audit and the proposal are all delivered in writing. If you'd rather talk it through, mention it in your message and we'll set up a call.

Do you work in French and English?

Yes. KORTEX produces content in French and English, so you can send a source in either language and choose the language of the assets.

Is there a long-term commitment?

No. GHOST, SWARM and TITAN are month-to-month plans with no long-term contract. The details are on the pricing page.
