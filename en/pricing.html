Scope

What is included in every plan, and what is not?

Every plan includes full ownership, no setup fees, production-ready assets, GEO engineering and your own founder voice. What KORTEX does not do is film your video, run your social accounts or buy media: you bring the source, we build the system.

✓ Full ownership

Every article, LinkedIn asset, FAQ block and strategic document belongs 100% to your brand. You own all intellectual capital from day one.

✓ No long-term contract

Month-to-month terms on all plans. No lock-in period. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel with 30 days' notice.

✓ No setup fees

Zero onboarding costs and no hidden fees. Your first invoice is for your plan cost only, nothing else.

✓ Turnkey delivery

Assets are production-ready: fully edited, formatted, schema-marked and ready to publish immediately.

✓ GEO engineered

Every asset is structured for AI citation: entity clarity, answer-first formatting and semantic density, designed for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

We extract your founder voice from the source video and apply it consistently across all assets. Everything sounds like you.

Not included in the plans

  • Recording the source. You supply the founder video, podcast or webinar; KORTEX processes it.

  • Guaranteed citations. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews are third-party systems. KORTEX engineers content to be citable; no one can guarantee a specific answer engine will cite it.
  • Plan-specific items. GHOST does not include FAQ clusters, newsletter restructuring, internal linking strategy, keyword clustering or strategy calls. SWARM does not include Slack access, a dedicated operator, citation tracking or competitive analysis.

How to think about ROI without inventing numbers

A GEO plan pays off when your brand starts appearing in the answers your buyers read before they ever visit your site. You can check that yourself, without any vanity metric:

  • Cost per asset. SWARM delivers 4 articles and 12–16 LinkedIn assets per cycle: €5,500 ÷ 16 to 20 assets is roughly €275–€345 per asset, before counting the schema, entity architecture and strategy work. GHOST: €2,500 ÷ 10 assets = €250 per asset.
  • Founder time. You record once; you do not write, edit or brief writers. Value the hours you get back at your own rate.
  • Shelf life. A LinkedIn post fades within days; a pillar article and FAQ cluster structured for answer engines stays retrievable for as long as it is accurate. Read why most B2B LinkedIn content dies for the mechanism.
  • Citation check. Before you start, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the ten questions your buyers ask. Repeat each month. That before/after is your clearest signal.

Pricing FAQ

How much does a GEO agency cost with KORTEX?

KORTEX plans cost €2,500 per month (GHOST), €5,500 per month (SWARM) and from €12,000 per month (TITAN). Annual pricing is 20% lower: €2,000, €4,400 and from €9,600 per month. There are no setup fees.

Are there any hidden fees or setup costs?

No. KORTEX pricing is fully transparent: zero setup fees, zero onboarding costs, zero hidden charges. Your invoice equals your plan cost. If you upgrade mid-month, we prorate fairly.

Can I change plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade to a higher plan immediately, downgrade at the start of the next billing cycle, or cancel with 30 days' notice. No penalties, no fine print.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. You can start with GHOST for one month, review the output and move to SWARM the next month, or start with SWARM and cancel if it is not right for you.

Who owns the content that KORTEX produces?

You do, 100%. Every article, FAQ block, LinkedIn post and strategic document belongs entirely to your brand from day one. KORTEX has no ownership stake in the content it produces. You can republish, repurpose or resell it without permission.

What if I need more than one source cycle per month?

GHOST and SWARM include one source cycle per month; TITAN includes two or more. If you need a different volume, contact KORTEX about custom volume pricing.

How long is the turnaround on each source cycle?

GHOST and SWARM: 5 business days from submission to delivery. TITAN: 3–4 business days with priority processing. Once submitted, you get progress updates and a final review before anything goes live.

What support do I get, and are revisions included?

GHOST and SWARM include email support with a 48-hour response. TITAN includes a dedicated Slack channel with a 24-hour response SLA. If an asset needs revision, KORTEX reworks it at no additional cost.

Does KORTEX guarantee that ChatGPT or Perplexity will cite my brand?

No. Answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews are third-party systems, so no agency can guarantee a citation. KORTEX engineers every asset to be citable, and TITAN includes a citation tracking dashboard to monitor results.

Can I request custom pricing or a trial period?

Yes, you can discuss custom pricing: write to contact@kortexlab.me with your situation. Every new inquiry can start with a free gap analysis, with no commitment and no pitch call required.
