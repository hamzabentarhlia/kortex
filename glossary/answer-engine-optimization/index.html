Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

In short

Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO, means shaping content so that it is chosen as the direct answer to a question, whether by a featured snippet in Google, a voice assistant or an AI chatbot. In practice AEO and GEO overlap heavily, and many people use the two terms for the same work.

AEO or GEO: which term is right?

AEO came first, when the goal was the featured snippet box at the top of Google. GEO appeared with generative AI engines that write their own answers. The techniques are largely shared: answer first, be specific, structure your content, and be consistent.
